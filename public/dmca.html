2. Filing a takedown notice

If you believe content on NVME infringes your copyright, send our agent a notice containing all of the following:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature (or that of a person authorized to act for you)
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed
  3. The URL(s) of the material on NVME you want removed, with enough detail for us to locate it
  4. Your name, address, telephone number, and email
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law
  6. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner

We review complete notices promptly and may remove or disable access to the material, notifying the uploader where possible.

3. Counter-notification

If your content was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may file a counter-notice containing:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature
  2. Identification of the removed material and where it appeared
  3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed by mistake or misidentification
  4. Your name, address, telephone number, and email, and consent to the jurisdiction of the courts of Bermuda (or, for U.S. residents, your federal judicial district)

Where the law requires, we may restore the material 10–14 business days after a valid counter-notice unless the complainant files a court action.

4. Repeat infringers

Accounts that receive multiple valid copyright complaints may be suspended or permanently terminated. We track complaints per account and act at our discretion, with termination the default for clear repeat infringement.

5. Music and sounds

Upload only sounds and music you own or are licensed to use. Platform-provided sounds may be used in videos created on NVME, within the platform only.

6. Good faith

Knowingly false takedown notices or counter-notices can create legal liability (including under 17 U.S.C. §512(f)). Please file carefully.
